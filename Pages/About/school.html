Who we are

Welcome to Adonis International School

Adonis International School is a government-approved school established in 2004, dedicated to providing quality education in a safe, nurturing, and inspiring environment.

We offer a complete educational journey through our Crèche, Nursery, Primary, and Secondary sections, helping children develop strong academic foundations, confidence, good character, creativity, leadership skills, and the ability to think independently.

With dedicated teachers and a child-centered approach to learning, we are committed to nurturing every child's unique potential and preparing them for academic success, responsible citizenship, and a bright future.

Our promise

Our Commitment to Excellence

At Adonis International School, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. True education should develop the whole child by combining academic knowledge with character, creativity, discipline, confidence, communication skills, and the ability to make responsible decisions.

Our commitment is to create an educational experience where every child is encouraged to learn, ask questions, discover new interests, overcome challenges, and develop the confidence to pursue their dreams.

We understand that every child is unique. For this reason, we encourage a learning environment that recognizes individual abilities, interests, learning needs, and areas for improvement. Our goal is to help each learner discover their strengths while receiving the guidance and support needed to grow.

Every stage

A Complete Educational Journey

From the earliest stages of childhood through secondary education, we provide a continuous learning journey designed to support children as they progress through different stages of development.

  1. 01

    Crèche

    Children meet the joy of learning through age-appropriate activities and meaningful interactions.

  2. 02

    Nursery

    Early literacy, numeracy and social skills grow through play, activity and discovery.

  3. 03

    Primary

    Stronger academic foundations, communication abilities, creativity and social skills.

  4. 04

    Secondary

    Students become more independent, analytical and responsible, ready for higher education and the wider world.

Through every stage, we aim to give learners the knowledge, values, and skills they need to become confident and responsible members of society.

Our values

Character and Moral Development

We believe that academic achievement is most meaningful when it is supported by good character.

  • Honesty
  • Respect
  • Responsibility
  • Discipline
  • Kindness
  • Integrity
  • Cooperation
  • Consideration

Our students are encouraged to understand that success is not only about academic performance but also about becoming people who can contribute positively to their families, communities, and society.

We therefore place strong emphasis on helping learners develop the attitudes and values required to become responsible and dependable citizens.

Growing together since 2004
At Adonis International School, we do more than teach children. We help them discover who they can become.

Since our establishment in 2004, we have remained committed to educating and nurturing young minds, guided by the belief that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, discover their abilities, and build a strong foundation for the future. As we look ahead, we remain committed to improving the educational experience of our learners while preserving the values that have guided us.

We welcome parents and guardians who share our commitment to giving their children a meaningful educational foundation and a brighter future.
